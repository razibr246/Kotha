# Returns and refunds

## Return policy
You can return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Clothing can be exchanged for another size within 7 days. Opened skincare and innerwear can't be returned.

## Damaged or wrong item
If your item arrives damaged or broken, or you got the wrong item, tell us within 48 hours with a photo. We pick it up for free and send a replacement or a full refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to your bKash or Nagad within 3-5 working days after we receive the returned item. Card refunds take 7-10 working days. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your bKash number.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel any order that hasn't shipped yet, free of charge. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled, but you can refuse it at the door or return it.
