# Payment

## Payment methods
We accept cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad and Visa or Mastercard. Cash on delivery is available across Bangladesh.

## Paying with bKash or Nagad
Choose bKash or Nagad at checkout and approve the payment in your app. Your order is confirmed as soon as the payment goes through.

## Payment failed but money was cut
If money left your account but the order shows unpaid, it is returned automatically within 24 hours. If it isn't, send us the transaction ID.
