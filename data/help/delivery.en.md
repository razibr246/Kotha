# Delivery

## Delivery time
Inside Dhaka city, orders arrive in 1-2 days after shipping. Outside Dhaka, delivery takes 3-5 days. Orders placed before 2 pm usually ship the same day.

## Delivery charge
Delivery inside Dhaka costs ৳70. Outside Dhaka it costs ৳130. Delivery is free on orders over ৳2,000.

## Couriers
We ship with Pathao inside Dhaka and Steadfast outside Dhaka. You get an SMS with your tracking number when your order ships.

## Missed delivery
If the rider can't reach you, they try again the next day. After two failed attempts the parcel comes back to us and you can ask us to reship it.
